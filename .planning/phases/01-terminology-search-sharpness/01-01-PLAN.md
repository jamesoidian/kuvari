<objective>
Align UI nomenclature with AAC standards (renaming "Category" to "Image Type" / "Kuvatyyppi") and render high-resolution symbols in the search results grid to eliminate blurriness on Retina screens.

Purpose: In AAC communication and Papunet's symbol services, "kategoria" refers to semantic content categories (food, clothing, feelings), whereas Kuvari's filters select image styles/symbol sets (arasaac, sclera, photo, drawing, etc.). Correcting this terminology prevents confusion for AAC professionals and users. Concurrently, rendering full-resolution symbols (`img.url`) rather than low-resolution thumbnails (`img.thumb`) ensures symbols remain crisp and clear when scaled up on modern mobile displays.
Output: Updated ARB files, regenerated localizations, sharp ImageGrid rendering, and updated widget tests.
</objective>
